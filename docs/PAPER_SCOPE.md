# Frozen contribution

The scientific scope is the exact finite, real, single-copy universally
unbiased readout limit, its sparse attaining measurement, and existing charged
local realizations. The overlap construction and fixed comparisons support it.
See [Scope](SCOPE.md), [Theory](THEORY.md), and [Comparisons](COMPARISONS.md).

The freeze excludes new ansätze, arbitrary-spectrum exact optima, complex/mixed
states, coherent reuse, optimizer convergence, hardware/noise/routing campaigns,
new compiler searches, larger sweeps, and favorable cost searches. Substantive
defects must be disclosed and contained; do not change assumptions to rescue a
claim without a separately justified decision.

The unmet end-to-end advantage milestone and unresolved novelty remain visible.
Consolidation and local corrections do not imply submission readiness.
Manuscript preparation is separate work.

The original scope record remains in [the source packet](../results/PF-08/DECISION.md)
and [archived guidance](../maintenance/repository-polish/previous_guidance/docs/PAPER_SCOPE.md).
