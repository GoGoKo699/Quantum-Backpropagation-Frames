## One question

What is the least possible single-copy statistical error for extracting the
complete gradient from a known reference and an unknown real quantum response,
and when can an attaining measurement be implemented without free basis access?

## One central claim

For equal nonzero tangent sensitivities, the exact universally unbiased
single-copy minimax trace risk is lambda*max(2r,4(r-1)). One joint measurement
with at most 2r+1 outcomes attains it. The existing one-layer local family has a
linear-size realization with all controls, preprocessing and output charged.
The bounds and their real/pure/unbiased/access hypotheses are inseparable.

This is the candidate theoretical contribution. It does NOT assert the first
optimal measurement for real states, generic backpropagation scaling, practical
quantum advantage, or optimal high-confidence sample complexity. The prior
source audit is insufficient for final novelty clearance.
