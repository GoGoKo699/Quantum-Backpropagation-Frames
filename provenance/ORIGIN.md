# Origin and terminology

Hopf-QBP was a source of inspiration for the initial discussion. It is not a
mathematical, software, or evidentiary dependency of this project. Results here
must follow from the assumptions, derivations, code, and scientific sources
actually identified in this repository.

The user clarified this positioning on 2026-09-15. Current descriptions and
scope therefore use the project's own question: frame-based complete-gradient
readout and the cost of compiling shared measurements.

The two input archives, their extracted research packets, and the original
bootstrap logs are retained byte-for-byte. Some contain historical comparison
labels or statements that another repository was not modified. Those labels
are provenance, not restrictions on the ansatz family or premises of a theorem.
Retaining them does not import results from that other project.

No scientific formula, source packet, or diagnostic result was changed by this
editorial clarification. Ordinary citations to genuinely relevant literature
remain necessary regardless of the history of the project.
