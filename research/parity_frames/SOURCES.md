# Primary sources consulted

- Park, Teo, Jeong, *Resource-efficient shadow tomography using equatorial
  stabilizer measurements*, arXiv:2311.14622v4.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2311.14622v4
  Supplies the established equatorial-stabilizer measurement context and the
  explicit O(n^2)-CZ realization. The covariance interpolation here is separately
  derived in PROOF.md, not attributed as a theorem in that article.
- Nakata and Murao, *Diagonal-unitary 2-designs and their implementations by
  quantum circuits*, arXiv:1206.4451.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4451
- Nakata, Koashi, Murao, *Generating a state t-design by diagonal quantum
  circuits*, arXiv:1311.1128.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.1128
  Relevant general design literature; no claim that the present ensemble is a
  full unitary or state design.
- Fischer, Dao, Tavernelli, Tacchino, *Dual frame optimization for informationally
  complete quantum measurements*, arXiv:2401.18071v2.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2401.18071v2
  Relevant decoder-optimization context; the fixed-measurement uniqueness lemma
  uses a different reference-restricted family and is derived directly.
- Li et al., *Efficient Quantum Gradient and Higher-order Derivative Estimation
  via Generalized Hadamard Test*, arXiv:2408.05406v1.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2408.05406v1
- Cleve, Leung, Liu, Wang, *Near-linear constructions of exact unitary 2-designs*,
  arXiv:1501.04592.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.04592
- Bertoni et al., *Shallow Shadows: Expectation Estimation Using Low-Depth Random
  Clifford Circuits*, arXiv:2209.12924v3; PRL 133, 020602 (2024).
  https://arxiv.org/html/2209.12924v3

The last two sources prevent an inference that quadratic gate counts are a
universal requirement for useful randomized measurement. No exhaustive novelty
or patent search was performed. Web search returned some unrelated results;
those are not used as evidence.
